<p><strong>ABOUT THE BRATTLE GROUP AND ECONOMIC CONSULTING</strong></p>
<p>The Brattle Group is a leading global economic and litigation consulting firm. We provide expert analysis and testimony in legal disputes, regulatory proceedings, and business strategy. Our teams apply economic, financial, and analytical expertise to solve complex problems and provide strategic insights for our clients. At The Brattle Group, we bridge the gap between economic theory and real-world application, helping clients make informed decisions in areas such as corporate finance, energy, competition, and regulatory economics.</p>
<hr>
<p><strong>WHAT IS ECONOMIC CONSULTING?</strong></p>
<p>Economic consulting involves using economic and financial theories, quantitative methods, and data analysis to address legal and business challenges. Consultants work on high-stakes projects, providing expert testimony and strategic advice to law firms, corporations, and government agencies. Our work influences major decisions and outcomes in legal cases and business strategies worldwide.</p>
<hr>
<p><strong>RESEARCH ANALYST ROLE</strong></p>
<p><strong>As a Research Analyst (RA) at The Brattle Group, you will be integral to our project teams, collaborating with senior consultants and experts. Your responsibilities will include:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Quantitative Models and Solutions:</strong> You'll build models and create innovative data-based solutions for clients using programming languages, such as R, Python, Stata, SAS, Excel, and VBA (e.g. conducting data and statistical analysis, econometric analysis, building interactive dashboards, etc.).</li>
<li><strong>Qualitative Research and Literature Review:</strong> You'll conduct comprehensive research, including market analysis, literature reviews, document examination, and writing memos to provide insights and support expert reports.</li>
<li><strong>Supporting Expert Witnesses:</strong> You'll assist in preparing expert reports and presentations, helping our experts present complex economic and financial concepts clearly in legal and regulatory contexts.</li>
<li><strong>Collaborating with Team Members:</strong> Effective communication and teamwork are critical. You'll work closely with colleagues at all levels, contributing to the development of robust and innovative solutions for our clients.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<p><strong>TYPICAL CASES IN METRICS</strong></p>
<p>In the METRICS practice, you will work on cases such as:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Antitrust &amp; Competition:</strong> Research markets, evaluate any restrictions to competition and, estimate any relevant damages.</li>
<li><strong>Consumer Protection and Product Liability:</strong> Identify the effects of misinformation on consumer choice, elicit the value that consumers place on particular product features through the statistical and econometric evaluation of sales data.</li>
<li><strong>Telecom:</strong> Conduct analysis related to competition, auctions, and mergers within the telecom industry.</li>
</ul>
