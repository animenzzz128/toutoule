<div class="content-intro"><h2><strong>Company Overview</strong></h2>
<p>ID.me is the next-generation digital identity wallet that simplifies how individuals securely prove their identity online. Consumers can verify their identity with ID.me once and seamlessly login across websites without having to create a new login and verify their identity again. Over 152 million users experience streamlined login and identity verification with ID.me at 20 federal agencies, 45 state government agencies, and 70+ healthcare organizations. More than 600+ consumer brands use ID.me to verify communities and user segments to honor service and build more authentic relationships. ID.me’s technology meets the federal standards for consumer authentication set by the Commerce Department and is approved as a NIST 800-63-3 IAL2 / AAL2 credential service provider by the Kantara Initiative. ID.me is committed to “No Identity Left Behind” to enable all people to have a secure digital identity. To learn more, visit&nbsp;<a href="https://network.id.me/">https://network.id.me/</a>.</p>

<p>As an Associate Product Manager, you will support the strategy and execution of platform initiatives that operate at internet scale, working alongside senior PMs to translate requirements into shipped features. This role balances user experience, technical feasibility, and cross-functional coordination as you build the foundational skills of product management on a platform used to power trusted digital experiences across industries.&nbsp;</p>
<p>This is a high-growth early-career role where you'll get hands-on ownership of discrete product surfaces, work closely with engineering, design, and data, and build toward increasing autonomy over roadmap and strategy.&nbsp;</p>
<p>This role is based out of our Mountain View, CA or McLean, VA offices and requires full-time in-office attendance.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Why This Role </strong></p>
<p>This role is a launchpad into product management at the intersection of technology, trust, and identity. You'll get direct ownership earlier than most APM programs, mentorship from senior platform PMs, and a clear growth path as you take on more strategic scope.</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Do</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Feature Ownership</strong>: Own the definition and delivery of discrete features or workflows within a larger platform roadmap, from requirements through launch.&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Execution Support</strong>: Break down roadmap priorities into clear, sequenced work; write specs and acceptance criteria that engineering and design can build against.&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Cross-Functional Collaboration</strong>: Partner day-to-day with engineering, design, and data to unblock delivery and ensure quality.&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Data &amp; Feedback</strong>: Track launch metrics and user feedback; use them to inform iteration and surface issues early.&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Stakeholder Communication</strong>: Keep your PM lead and cross-functional partners informed on status, risks, and trade-offs.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
