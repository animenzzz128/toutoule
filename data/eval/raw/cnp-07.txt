<p><strong><span data-contrast="auto">Position Summary</span></strong><span data-ccp-props="{&quot;201341983&quot;:0,&quot;335559739&quot;:0,&quot;335559740&quot;:240}">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span data-contrast="auto">We’re seeking a full-time Associate Buyer for our&nbsp;</span><span data-contrast="auto">Los Angeles-based </span><span data-contrast="auto">corporate office. They will be a</span>&nbsp;highly motivated, adaptable Associate Buyer. This position offers long-term growth potential compared to a traditional production coordinator role. The ideal candidate will be trained from the ground up on core merchandising and visual workflows.</p>
<p>The Associate Buyer will begin by supporting the Senior Buyer, learning SHEIN’s buying processes and merchandising approach through hands-on involvement, with opportunities to take on greater ownership and responsibility over time.&nbsp;The Associate Buyer will coordinate the end-to-end sample lifecycle, assist with product styling and outfit coordination, build photoshoot presentations, and manage new item launch linking systems. This role serves as a dynamic operational backbone, supporting administrative and logistical workflows to ensure product readiness, data accuracy, and visually compelling launches.</p>
<p><strong><span data-contrast="auto">Job Responsibilities</span></strong></p>
<p><span data-ccp-props="{&quot;134233117&quot;:true,&quot;134233118&quot;:true,&quot;201341983&quot;:0,&quot;335559740&quot;:240}">Styling &amp; Photoshoot Support&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></p>
<ul>
<li>Trend &amp; Styling Alignment: Assist the Senior Buyer with daily product styling, outfit combinations, and visual coordination, developing a strong eye for trend execution from the ground up.</li>
<li>Photoshoot Asset Preparation: Prepare the photoshoot presentation (PPT) at least one week in advance, including finalized product lists and curated outfit pairings for manager review.</li>
<li>Studio Coordination: Coordinate with the Creative and Studio teams to schedule and prepare for upcoming photoshoots based on incoming sample arrivals.</li>
<li>Sample Readiness: Ensure all clothing, shoes, and accessory samples required for photoshoots are organized, labeled, and tracked to ensure they are ready for on-set utilization.</li>
<li>Studio Alignment: Partner with the Creative and Studio teams to ensure the photoshoot schedule runs smoothly without delays.</li>
<li>Issue Resolution: Track and communicate any missing or delayed samples to the relevant teams promptly, adjusting shoot schedules as needed to minimize production downtime.</li>
</ul>
<p>New Item Launch &amp; Linking Management</p>
<ul>
<li>Launch Schedule Oversight: Monitor the weekly new item launching schedule to ensure all SKC targets are met accurately and on time.</li>
<li>Weekly Plan Submissions: Submit the comprehensive launch plan every Wednesday for the following week, integrating new items, recolors, and required outfit linkings.</li>
<li>Pre-Launch Readiness Audits: Review each day’s launch readiness at least one business day prior to going live, double-checking product linking, imagery, measurements, and inventory.</li>
<li>Data Correction: Actively identify and communicate linking errors, missing measurements, or readiness concerns to backend teams before strict deadlines.</li>
<li>Tracking &amp; Reporting: Maintain and update launch trackers with real-time status updates, delays, and revised timelines.</li>
<li>Goal Alignment: Align weekly and monthly launch progress with merchandising goals to share reports with management.</li>
</ul>
<p>Sample Room Operations &amp; Inventory Control</p>
<ul>
<li>Sample Intake &amp; Labeling: Coordinate the receiving, labeling, organization, and categorization of all seasonal and recutting samples.</li>
<li>Systematic Inventory Categorization: Maintain the sample room inventory structure by grouping styles systematically by category (e.g., tops, dresses, bottoms) and specific launch periods.</li>
<li>Purging &amp; Maintenance: Conduct routine audits to identify and remove outdated, damaged, or duplicate samples to optimize space.</li>
<li>Sample Tracking &amp; Accountability: Organize, label, and track samples needed for photoshoots and product launches to support sample readiness and accountability.</li>
<li>Photo Documentation: Provide weekly progress updates and clean sample room photo documentation every Friday by 4:00 PM.</li>
</ul>
<p>Cross-Functional Coordination &amp; Workflow Optimization</p>
<ul>
<li>Communication Bridge: Serve as a key point of contact between Merchandising, Creative/Studio, and backend teams to keep product data, measurements, samples, and timelines aligned.</li>
<li>Risk Mitigation: Track and report operational bottlenecks that may delay product launches or shoots, proactively proposing flexible corrective actions.</li>
<li>Digital Archiving: Maintain impeccably organized shared folders and documentation for weekly and monthly launch history records.</li>
<li>Process Efficiency: Support continuous improvement by suggesting workflow efficiencies across launch operations and sample management.</li>
</ul>

<li>Proficient in Microsoft Excel, shared drive management, and basic product tracking systems.</li>
<li>Strong foundational interest in fashion trends, commercial styling, and visual presentation.</li>
<li>Exceptional attention to detail, rigorous organizational habits, and strong communication skills.</li>
<li>Ability to multitask, manage moving parts, and meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment.</li>
<li>Ability to work collaboratively throughout the organization.</li>
<li>Ability to effectively communicate with all levels of staff and management.</li>
<li>Thrives in a fast-paced environment with short timelines and changing priorities.</li>
<li>Strong sense of urgency balanced with a highly meticulous approach to data and deadlines.</li>
<li>Collaborative, proactive, self-motivated, and comfortable taking ownership of assigned responsibilities.</li>
<li>A positive, motivated individual who is comfortable troubleshooting issues and supporting changing business priorities.</li>
</ul>
