Responsibilities

TikTok Shop is redefining how people shop for the things they love through a unique discovery e-commerce model that brings together joy, entertainment, and commerce in one seamless experience.

About the Role
The TikTok Shop Graduate Development Program is a curated management trainee program designed for high-potential Bachelor's degree graduates who are excited about building the future of e-commerce in Southeast Asia. The program offers an accelerated pathway for early career talent to develop commercial judgment, analytical capability, operational excellence, and cross-functional leadership in one of the fastest-evolving digital commerce ecosystems.

From Day 1, you will take on meaningful business responsibilities through curated rotations across core e-commerce teams. Each rotation is designed to give you hands-on exposure to how we grow sellers, engage creators, run large scale campaigns, improve platform operations, and solve complex business problems.

Upon successful completion of the program, graduates will be well-positioned to take on a high-impact role with continued opportunities to grow as future business, operations, or strategy leaders.

Rotation Opportunities
Acquisition and Incubation: Support sellers from launch to early growth through acquisition, onboarding, training, and incubation. You will use data-driven insights, practical playbooks, and scalable mechanisms to help sellers succeed on the platform.

Category and Creator Growth: Work with creators, KOLs, sellers, and category teams to strengthen partnerships, improve product discovery, and drive content-led commerce growth.

Campaign Management: Contribute to campaigns that build awareness, engagement, and GMV growth, supporting strategy, execution, performance tracking, and post-campaign insights.

Platform Operations and Business Intelligence: Use analytics, process improvement, and project execution to solve platform-level challenges, improve operational efficiency, and support better business outcomes.

You will rotate across 3 to 4 teams during the program, with rotation length shaped by business needs. Your preferences, strengths, and development goals will be considered to create a meaningful and immerseive learning experience.

Selection Process
Online Assessment - Test your logical thinking, data interpretation, and structured problem-solving skills.
HR Team Interview - Share your motivation, experiences, career interests, and fit for the graduate development program.
Case Assessments - Work through real-world e-commerce business scenarios to demonstrate analytical thinking, business judgment, and communication skills.
Business Leaders Interview - Meet with business leaders to discuss your motivations, problem-solving approach, leadership potential, and readiness to grow in a fast-paced e-commerce environment
