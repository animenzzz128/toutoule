<p><strong>A Day In The Life of this Role at Momentum</strong></p>
<p>As an <strong>Associate Strategy &amp; Operations Analyst</strong>, your days will be dynamic and filled with opportunities to contribute to our mission. Here's a glimpse into what your typical day might look like:</p>
<ul>
<li>Coordinate and support high-priority projects, ensuring timely delivery and adapting to the dynamic needs of multiple portfolio companies</li>
<li>Analyze datasets to guide business case creation and deliver recommendations to stakeholders&nbsp;</li>
<li>Evaluate workflow inefficiencies and help create operational frameworks to optimize internal procedures.</li>
<li>Monitor and communicate essential project milestones while ensuring adherence to risk mitigation policies.</li>
<li>Support initiative workshops by helping build slide decks, and reliably distributing agendas and post-meeting summaries.</li>
<li>Assist in building internal knowledge, AI-powered tools, and automation workflows that enhance the company's capabilities.</li>
<li>Continuously seek ways to leverage AI and automation to innovate processes, implement best practices, and drive efficiency gains.</li>
<li>Partner with key stakeholders across the portfolio to ensure project alignment with business objectives and strategy.</li>
<li>Craft clear and concise communications to stakeholders of different work and communication styles.</li>
<li>Build and maintain strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders, working cross-functionally to drive project success and process improvement.</li>
<li>Support the design, planning, and execution of strategic projects, ensuring all deliverables align with stakeholders.</li>
<li>Attend training sessions to contribute to your professional growth and development.</li>

<p>We’re looking for someone who:</p>
<ul>
