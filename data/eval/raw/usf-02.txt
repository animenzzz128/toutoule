<p>As an Investment Intern in Robinhood Ventures you will be a core part of our lean team. You will research, evaluate, and support investments across the Robinhood Ventures platform, with a focus on late-stage investments.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.&nbsp;</strong></p>

<h2><strong>What you’ll do</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Research and sourcing </strong>- You’ll research markets and sectors that you believe we should be paying attention to, and then identify the key companies in them that Robinhood Ventures needs to meet.<strong>&nbsp;</strong></li>
<li><strong>Investment evaluation </strong>- You’ll be a key part of the deal team for investments we evaluate. You’ll own core diligence workstreams like the financial model, competitive analysis, and help shape investment theses.&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Platform building </strong>- You’ll work with teams across Product, Operations, Marketing, Legal, and more to help build experiences in the Robinhood app to further differentiate Robinhood Ventures for both retail investors and portfolio companies.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>What you bring</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li>Currently enrolled in a full-time MBA program&nbsp;</li>
<li>Prior experience in investment banking, private equity, or finance at a high-growth company, with fluency in financial modeling, valuation analysis, and market research; late-stage investing or venture capital experience is a plus</li>
<li>Intellectual curiosity and a demonstrated interest in emerging technologies and the private companies driving them — you proactively form opinions on key trends and companies</li>
<li>An existing network in the startup ecosystem, or the drive to build one, that can help Robinhood Ventures identify and connect with promising companies</li>
<li>Proven ability to work independently in lean, high-output environments, with experience collaborating across cross-functional teams</li>
</ul>
