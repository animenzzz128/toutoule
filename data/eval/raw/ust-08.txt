## Description

### About CWILL

CWILL (pronounced 'quill') is a post-purchase and retention suite built for Shopify brands. Reduce support tickets, recover lost revenue from returns, and turn one-time buyers into loyal fans — with tools purpose-built for every touchpoint that follows the sale.

### About the Role

As our product portfolio grows, we need new product talent that can learn fast, stay close to users, and help turn real customer problems into great product experiences. Our Product Management Trainee program places you inside one of our product teams, where you'll learn the full PM workflow through real work — not slides and simulations. You'll start by supporting core product tasks, and over time grow into someone who can independently own a product module or direction.

You'll be placed on one of our active product teams:

-   Order Tracking: post-purchase shipment visibility and tracking features for Shopify brands
-   AI Chat: AI customer service, Live Chat, Shopify order/product integrations, knowledge base, and merchant dashboard

**Base location required in 5 states:**

CA, NC, IL, TX, NJ

(we unable to hire in other states, relocation will be required)

### What You'll Do

**Product Requirement Analysis & Solution Design**

-   Collect and analyze user feedback, customer requests, product data, and market information to identify product improvement opportunities
-   Participate in feature planning discussions — ideation, user flows, and solution design
-   Under PM guidance, produce requirement documents, user flows, wireframes, functional specs, and acceptance criteria
-   Track features from discovery through design, engineering, and launch — make sure problems are clearly defined and solutions land correctly

**Product Iteration**

-   Monitor product data and user feedback after launch; surface issues and propose next improvements

**User & Market Research**

-   Join user interviews and feedback sessions with Shopify merchants and ecommerce brands
-   Track competitors in your product's category and produce structured competitive analysis
-   Convert user insights and market signals into actionable product recommendation

**Cross-Team Collaboration & Execution**

-   Work closely with engineering, design, QA, operations, and customer success to drive high-quality product delivery
-   Proactively track project progress and flag issues early
-   Own your tasks with a results mindset — follow through on commitments and complete retrospectives after launch

**Continuous PM Development**

-   Learn product planning, user research, data analysis, and project management through real work
-   Conduct retrospectives on the features you ship; build your product judgment through iteration
-   As you grow, take on increasing responsibility — eventually owning a product module or workstream independently
